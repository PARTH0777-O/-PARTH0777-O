## 👋 About Me

I'm Parth — a full-stack developer who likes building products end-to-end: from an NLP model that parses a resume, to the API that serves it, to the dashboard that displays it. Most of my recent work sits at the intersection of **web development** and **applied AI** — resume analyzers, university-discovery platforms, AI call-QA tools, and AI-companion apps.

- 🔭 Currently building **AI-powered platforms** (resume analysis, call-QA scoring, university discovery)
- 🌱 Deepening my skills in **NLP** and **LLM-backed applications**
- 🛠️ Comfortable across the stack: **React/TypeScript** on the front, **Flask/FastAPI** on the back, **MongoDB/SQLite** underneath
- 🎯 Currently focusing on shipping polished, real-world-ready projects
- 💬 Ask me about React, Flask, FastAPI, or NLP pipelines
